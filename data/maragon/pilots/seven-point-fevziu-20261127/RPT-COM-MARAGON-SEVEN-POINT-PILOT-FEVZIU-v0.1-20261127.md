## 2. Evidence base

**Primary sample (sets the bands).** 24 full «Opinion» episodes were drawn at random (seed **20261127**) from a frame of 55 air dates between 28.05.2026 and 26.11.2026. Each episode was the longest upload for its air date in the official RTV KLAN playlists "OPINION – TV KLAN | 2026-2027" and "Opinion | TV Klan 2025-2026". There are no episodes from 10.06 to 02.09.2026, because the host said on air (03.09.2026) that the programme was off for the World Cup. All **24 transcripts were obtained**: YouTube auto-generated Albanian captions, fetched on 27.11.2026 with youtube-transcript-api and yt-dlp, with backoff after YouTube rate limits. The S1 gate for a daily-format programme (≥ 20 random episodes over ≥ 6 months, ruling of 19.11.2026) is met on count and span.

**Coding.**
- **Q-units:** 288 candidate host questions were drawn, 12 per episode with seed `20261127-<videoId>`. 197 could be attributed to the host. 91 were dropped because the captions have no speaker labels. Of the 197, 113 are public-affairs questions and 84 come from feature segments (hotels, shops, sport, personal stories).
- **M-units:** 108 opening-monologue segments were coded, segments 2–6 of each episode. 89 of them, across 23 episodes, contain a factual assertion by the host.
- **Who coded:** one AI coder (Grok Bot). There was no second coder and no inter-rater check. The codes are in `raw/qcodes_b*.txt` and `raw/mcodes.txt`.

**Secondary in-window sample (27.11.2023–26.05.2026).** 12 episodes were drawn (seed 20261128). **None were transcribed:** the caption fetch failed three times each for the first two (Dec 2023 and Jan 2024 episodes), and I stopped it to finish the pilot. Older in-window evidence comes instead from AMA records, fact-checkers and the press, and each item is dated below.

**Accounts.** Only Instagram @blendifevziu is verified in the 27.11.2026 register. Its content sits behind a login wall and was **not read**. X @BlendiFevziu, YouTube @BlendiFevziuofficial and Facebook are UNCERTAIN, so they were excluded. No account was counted on a name match.

**Earlier material.** The live-scoring v0.2 dossier (AMA census, re-checked 21 and 27.11.2026) is reused only for the structural side of P1.

- **The lower of the two is 41–60.** Sampling error does not move it: even at the upper CI bound (12.5%), the result stays in 41–60.

## 6. Go / no-go and what would be needed

**NO-GO.** Only 2 of the 7 points were scored, against a threshold of 5. The failed points and the evidence each lacks are in §4. To make a seven-point exercise feasible:
1. Owner decisions: an approved rubric for P2 and P6, which means reversing D-06/D-07 for a v2.0; whether feature segments count for P5; and whether event footage counts as an "on-screen document" for P7.
2. Diarised transcripts. Speaker separation, or human-checked transcripts, would unlock P4 and recover the 32% of question units lost to attribution.
3. A claim-verification pass for P3 (≥ 60 claims) and an accuracy check of context items for P5.
4. A second coder on at least 20% of units, to test reliability.
5. Access to verified social accounts (logged-in reading of Instagram @blendifevziu), if posts are to count.

The pipeline is reusable, but **no rollout is prepared or run**, because the decision is no-go:
- `build_frame.py`: sampling frame.
- `sample_and_fetch.py` and `fetch_ytdlp.py`: seeded sample and caption fetch with backoff.
- `extract_units.py`: unit extraction.
- `build_pilot.py`: builds the JSON from the codes.
