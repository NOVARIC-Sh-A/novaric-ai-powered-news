## §6.2 step 4 — replacement wording (OD-S13)

Ruled 27.11.2026 22:30/22:31.

P2 and P6 have a calibration cap of 24 hours per profile. The cap starts when that profile’s first seven-point score is generated. After the cap, P2 and P6 display with a DRAFT/provisional label.

Until the cap ends, the overall result is the mean of the five points P1, P3, P4, P5 and P7 (OD-S10). After the cap, the overall result is the mean of all seven. If any point in the active set is missing, the overall stays Pa vlerësim. The mean is shown as a band, never as a single number.

The evidence sample window is 18 months (OD-S11).

Any public value carries the DRAFT label and the AM-09 provisional label (PROVIZOR · në shqyrtim / PROVISIONAL · under review). The FEVZIU cells are additionally marked PILOT · DRAFT.
